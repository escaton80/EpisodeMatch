IMPORTANT — GPL COMPONENTS ARE NOT PART OF THIS ARCHIVE
-------------------------------------------------------
The external tools ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe, mkvmerge.exe, mkvextract.exe,
mkvpropedit.exe (GPL) and seconv.exe (GPL, part of SubtitleEdit) are NOT
included in this archive and are NOT distributed by EpisodeMatch. On first
run, the built-in downloader fetches them locally on YOUR machine directly
from their original distribution servers (gyan.dev, mkvtoolnix.download,
github.com/SubtitleEdit). You are the downloader of those binaries; please
observe the respective licenses (GPLv2/GPLv3). Their complete source code is
available from the projects' official repositories.
